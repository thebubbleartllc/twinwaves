Support

Twin Waves is a synthesizer for iPhone that draws every note of your chord as a glowing line. If something isn't working or you have a question, we're happy to help.

Contact us

Email matt@thebubbleart.com. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version, and what you were doing when the problem happened. We usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

I don't hear any sound

  • Turn up the volume and check that your iPhone isn't on silent in a way that mutes media.
  • If headphones or a speaker are connected, make sure sound is going to them.
  • Close Twin Waves completely and open it again.

A note keeps playing

Tap ALL OFF. It stops every note, the loop and the drums. If HOLD or SUSTAIN is on, notes keep ringing until you turn them off.

How do I get my recordings onto a computer?

Open the TAPES screen. Use the share button to AirDrop, save or send a take. On a Mac you can also connect your iPhone by cable and open Finder → your iPhone → Files → Twin Waves. Recordings are 24-bit WAV files.

How do I record a loop and layer over it?

Tap LOOP, play four bars after the count-in, and the loop starts repeating. Pick another sound and tap LOOP again to add a layer. Snap keeps each layer on the beat, and you can change it in the ••• menu.

I got a new iPhone. Do I need to buy it again?

No. Download Twin Waves again from the App Store with the same Apple Account and it won't charge you again.
