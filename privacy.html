Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026

The short version: Twin Waves does not collect, store, share or sell any information about you. Everything you make stays on your iPhone.

Who we are

Twin Waves is published by The Bubble Art LLC ("we", "us"). This policy covers the Twin Waves app for iPhone.

What the app collects

Nothing. Twin Waves has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The app does not connect to the internet and does not send any data to us or to anyone else.

What stays on your device

Your sounds, saved presets, drum kits, loops, settings and recordings (Tapes) are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. We cannot see or access them. You can delete them in the app or by deleting the app. Your iPhone may include this app data in your own iCloud or computer backups, which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Sharing your recordings

If you choose to share a recording, for example with AirDrop or Messages, it goes only where you send it, using Apple's standard sharing tools. We don't receive a copy.

Purchases and diagnostics

Twin Waves is sold through the Apple App Store. Apple processes your purchase, and we do not receive your payment details. If you have chosen to share diagnostics with app developers in your iPhone settings, Apple may provide us with anonymous crash reports. This is controlled by you and Apple, under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Children

Twin Waves does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we ever change how Twin Waves handles data, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect.
